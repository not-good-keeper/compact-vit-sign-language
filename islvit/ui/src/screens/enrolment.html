Target for the team: 3 signers × 50 words × 3 takes ≈ 450 clips.

For a usable take

  • Start and end with your hands at rest, still for about half a second.
  • One sign per take, two to four seconds.
  • Keep your head and both hands in the frame the whole time.
  • Hold the camera landscape. A phone or webcam is fine.
  • Change the room, light or clothes between signers. The model has only seen one studio, so variety is the point.

Takes are saved per signer, so a test set can hold out people the model never saw.
